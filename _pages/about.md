# Junteng Liu

## About Me

I am a first-year PhD candidate at the HKUST NLP Group, under Professor Junxian He's supervision. I obtained my B.Eng. degree in June 2024 from Shanghai Jiao Tong University. My primary research focuses on natural language processing and machine learning.

### Research Interests

- **LLM Reasoning and Reinforcement Learning**
- **Hallucination in Vision-Language Models (VLM)**
- **LLM Truthfulness and Interpretability**
